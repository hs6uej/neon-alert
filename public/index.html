How to play

Economy

Build a Fusion Reactor first (power), then an Ore Processor — it comes with a free Nano Harvester that mines the green crystals. Low power slows production and shuts down towers and the radar.

Base & army

Click a structure in the sidebar, wait for READY, then click it again and place it next to your base. Barracks train infantry, the Assembly Forge builds vehicles & drones. The Sensor Array gives you the minimap and advanced units, the Quantum Lab unlocks elite tech and the Orbital Uplink superweapon.

Controls

LMB select / drag-box · RMB move / attack / mine · A attack-move · S stop · D deploy MCV · H home · Space last alert · Ctrl+1-9 set group, 1-9 recall · R repair · X sell · Tab next build tab · Enter chat · Arrow keys / screen edge / MMB-drag to scroll, wheel to zoom. Shift-click a card to queue 5.

Tips

Breach Engineers capture enemy buildings. Right-click a Barracks / Forge to set a rally point. Rocket Lancers and Pulse Towers hit air. Rail Strikers wreck armor.
